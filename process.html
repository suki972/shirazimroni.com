My process

Seven steps, thoughtfully sequenced.

From the first conversation to the day you settle in, every project follows a clear, collaborative path — designed to make the journey feel as considered as the result.

Engagement
Full home renovations · single rooms · move-in styling
Includes
Design, technical drawings, sourcing, coordination, oversight, final styling
  1. Step 01

    Initial Consultation

    Every project begins with getting to know our clients, their lifestyle, needs, and vision for the space. During the initial meeting, we discuss goals, functionality, aesthetic direction, budget, and overall expectations in order to create a strong foundation for the project.

    Step 01 · outcome
  2. Step 02

    Site Measurements & Space Assessment

    Accurate measurements and a detailed understanding of the existing space are essential to the design process. At this stage, we evaluate the layout, architectural conditions, technical constraints, and opportunities within the home.

    Step 02 · outcome
  3. Step 03

    Concept Development & Design Direction

    Following the site assessment, we begin developing the design concept and exploring layout possibilities, materials, finishes, and overall aesthetic direction. Clients are presented with curated options tailored to their needs and lifestyle, allowing the design vision to take shape.

    Step 03 · outcome
  4. Step 04

    Detailed Design & Visualization

    Once the design direction is approved, the project moves into a more detailed planning phase. This may include custom cabinetry design, elevations, space planning, material selections, and 3D visualizations that help bring the project to life and provide a clear understanding of the final result.

    Step 04 · outcome
  5. Step 05

    Technical Plans & Project Coordination

    After finalizing the design, detailed technical drawings and specifications are prepared for contractors and trades. This stage may include construction drawings, electrical and lighting plans, custom millwork details, and coordination with vendors and project professionals.

    Step 05 · outcome
  6. Step 06

    Material Selections & Ordering

    We guide clients through the selection of finishes, fixtures, furnishings, and materials to ensure a cohesive and thoughtful result. Depending on the project scope, this stage may include sourcing tile, plumbing fixtures, lighting, furniture, textiles, and accessories.

    Step 06 · outcome
  7. Step 07

    Project Execution & Styling

    During construction and installation, we work closely with contractors and vendors to ensure the design is executed as intended. The final stage includes styling, furnishing, and finishing touches that transform the space into a refined, functional, and fully realized home.

    Step 07 · outcome
