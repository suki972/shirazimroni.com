About me

Interiors that feel elevated, livable, and personal.

I'm an Austin-based interior designer specializing in residential renovations, custom interiors, and thoughtfully designed living spaces. My work is rooted in creating homes that balance beauty and function while reflecting how each family truly lives.

Before transitioning into interior design, I worked as an HR Business Partner in the startup world, where I developed a strong foundation in communication, project management, and understanding people's needs. That experience continues to shape the way I guide clients through the renovation process — with clarity, collaboration, and attention to detail from concept through completion.

While pursuing interior design studies at UC Berkeley, I began working in the design field on a range of residential projects, including full-home renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels, custom millwork, furnishings, and styling. My approach is warm, intentional, and detail-driven, with a strong focus on thoughtful design choices and creating spaces that feel cohesive, inviting, and personal.

Services

Three ways I work with clients.

  • 01

    Full Home Renovations

    Comprehensive design services for clients looking to renovate and transform their entire home with a cohesive vision, thoughtful planning, tailored design direction, project coordination and ongoing oversight.

  • 02

    Room & Space Renovations

    Focused design services for specific areas of the home, including kitchens, bathrooms, fireplaces, staircases, closets, offices, custom cabinetry, and built-ins.

  • 03

    Home Styling & Furnishing

    Furnishing, accessorizing, and styling services for clients looking to complete their home, refresh a room, or create a move-in-ready space.
