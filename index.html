Interiors that feel elevated, livable, and deeply personal.

Shira Zimroni is an interior designer specializing in residential renovations, custom interiors, and thoughtfully curated living spaces. Based in Austin, Texas, she lives with her husband and two daughters and brings a warm, lifestyle-driven approach to every project she takes on.

Before transitioning into interior design, Shira spent years working as an HR Business Partner within startup companies, where she developed a strong foundation in communication, project management, and understanding people's needs. Although she built a successful corporate career, her passion for interiors, architecture, and creating meaningful spaces ultimately led her to pursue design professionally.

After studying interior design in Berkeley, California, Shira began working on residential renovation and styling projects across the United States and abroad. Her work ranges from full-home remodels and kitchen and bathroom renovations to custom cabinetry, staircases, fireplaces, furnishings, and styling.

Shira's design philosophy centers around creating homes that feel elevated yet livable — spaces that are both beautiful and deeply personal. She believes thoughtful design has the ability to shape everyday life, bringing comfort, functionality, and a sense of connection to the home.

Known for her collaborative approach and attention to detail, Shira works closely with each client to create interiors that reflect their lifestyle, needs, and aesthetic in a timeless and intentional way.

Services

Four ways I work with clients.

Whether you're renovating a whole home or styling a single room, every engagement is tailored to your scope, timeline, and lifestyle.

  1. 01

    Full Home Renovations

    Comprehensive interior renovation services for clients looking to transform their homes from the ground up. Services include space planning, kitchens, bathrooms, custom architectural details, finish selections, and collaboration with contractors and trades to create cohesive, functional, and elevated living spaces.

    • Space planning
    • Kitchens & baths
    • Architectural detail
    • Finishes
    • Trade coordination
  2. 02

    Kitchens, Bathrooms & Custom Built-Ins

    Focused renovation and design services for individual spaces and custom features throughout the home. This includes kitchens, bathrooms, staircases, fireplaces, cabinetry, closets, offices, media walls, and outdoor kitchens, with an emphasis on thoughtful design and tailored functionality.

    • Kitchens
    • Bathrooms
    • Cabinetry
    • Closets & offices
    • Media walls
    • Outdoor kitchens
  3. 03

    Furnishing, Styling & Move-In Services

    Design support for furnishing and styling homes, whether for a single room or an entire home. Ideal for clients relocating from another city or country, this service includes furniture sourcing, layout planning, lighting, rugs, artwork, accessories, and creating a polished, move-in-ready home.

    • Furniture sourcing
    • Layout planning
    • Lighting
    • Rugs & textiles
    • Art & accessories
  4. 04

    Interior Design & Concept Development

    Design services focused on creating a clear vision and direction for a space. Includes room design, concept creation, material and finish selections, color palettes, furniture planning, and overall aesthetic development to ensure a cohesive and personalized result.

    • Concept creation
    • Material & finish selection
    • Color palettes
    • Furniture planning
    • Aesthetic direction
