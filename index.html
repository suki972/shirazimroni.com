Interiors that feel elevated, livable, and deeply personal.

I am an Austin-based interior designer specializing in residential renovations, custom interiors, and thoughtfully curated living spaces. My work focuses on creating homes that feel elevated, functional, and deeply personal, balancing beauty with the way each family truly lives.

Before transitioning into interior design, I worked as an HR Business Partner in the startup world, where I developed a strong foundation in communication, project management, and understanding people's needs. That experience continues to shape the way I work today, allowing me to guide clients through the design and renovation process with clarity, collaboration, and attention to detail.

After studying interior design in Berkeley, California, I began working on renovation and styling projects that range from full-home remodels and kitchen and bathroom renovations to custom cabinetry, staircases, fireplaces, furnishings, and styling. My approach is warm, intentional, and detail-driven, with the goal of creating timeless spaces that feel both beautiful and truly livable.

Services

Four ways I work with clients.

Whether you're renovating a whole home or styling a single room, every engagement is tailored to your scope, timeline, and lifestyle.

  1. 01

    Full Home Renovations

    Comprehensive interior renovation services for clients looking to transform their homes from the ground up. Services include space planning, kitchens, bathrooms, custom architectural details, finish selections, and collaboration with contractors and trades to create cohesive, functional, and elevated living spaces.

    • Space planning
    • Kitchens & baths
    • Architectural detail
    • Finishes
    • Trade coordination
  2. 02

    Kitchens, Bathrooms & Custom Built-Ins

    Focused renovation and design services for individual spaces and custom features throughout the home. This includes kitchens, bathrooms, staircases, fireplaces, cabinetry, closets, offices, media walls, and outdoor kitchens, with an emphasis on thoughtful design and tailored functionality.

    • Kitchens
    • Bathrooms
    • Cabinetry
    • Closets & offices
    • Media walls
    • Outdoor kitchens
  3. 03

    Furnishing, Styling & Move-In Services

    Design support for furnishing and styling homes, whether for a single room or an entire home. Ideal for clients relocating from another city or country, this service includes furniture sourcing, layout planning, lighting, rugs, artwork, accessories, and creating a polished, move-in-ready home.

    • Furniture sourcing
    • Layout planning
    • Lighting
    • Rugs & textiles
    • Art & accessories
  4. 04

    Interior Design & Concept Development

    Design services focused on creating a clear vision and direction for a space. Includes room design, concept creation, material and finish selections, color palettes, furniture planning, and overall aesthetic development to ensure a cohesive and personalized result.

    • Concept creation
    • Material & finish selection
    • Color palettes
    • Furniture planning
    • Aesthetic direction
