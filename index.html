About Shira

A Passion for Beautiful, Livable Spaces

I'm Shira Kohn Zimroni, an interior designer based in Austin, Texas, with a deep belief that great design should make your life better — not just look better.

With years of experience transforming homes across the Austin area, I bring a thoughtful, collaborative approach to every project. My work blends timeless aesthetics with modern functionality, always keeping your lifestyle at the center of every decision.

From initial concept to the final styling details, I'm with you every step of the way — making the process as enjoyable as the result.

The Design Process

A clear, collaborative journey from first conversation to final reveal.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We begin with a conversation — about your life, your style, your must-haves and your dreams. I listen before I design anything.

  2. 02

    Concept Design

    Floor plans, mood boards, material palettes, and furniture selections — a complete design vision tailored to you.

  3. 03

    Execution

    I coordinate vendors, contractors, and deliveries — managing every detail so the process is smooth and stress-free for you.

  4. 04

    Final Reveal

    The moment of transformation — your space, fully realized. Every piece in place, every detail considered, exactly as imagined.
